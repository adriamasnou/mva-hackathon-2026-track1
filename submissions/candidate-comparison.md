# Candidate comparison: what would change our choice?

**24 October 2026. Evidence comparison and proposed experiments. No head-to-head N1002K rescue experiment has been performed.**

Arimoclomol is our conditional preclinical lead because it combines a testable stress-response hypothesis with a documented human pharmacokinetic and regulatory record. Lead status is a provisional programme choice. That rationale does **not** establish that it is more likely to rescue N1002K than 4-PBA or NMN. NMN has stronger direct evidence of increased BubR1 abundance in the published systems below. The defensible next decision is a common functional comparison after the variant model qualifies, with permission to replace or abandon the lead. [Main report](adriamasnou_track2_report.md); [functional protocol](functional-rescue-plan.md). **[OUR INFERENCE; PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

## The same questions for each candidate

For each candidate, distinguish: what the primary experiment actually measured; whether it tested mutant BubR1 function; what human exposure can and cannot support; and which adverse result would stop the programme. An increase in Hsp70, an increase in BubR1 and improved segregation are three different endpoints. Clinical experience in another disease establishes neither MVA efficacy nor acceptable risk for this child. We do not assign numerical efficacy scores to incomparable experiments. **[OUR INFERENCE]**

### Arimoclomol: a defined translation record, an indirect BubR1 hypothesis

- **Positive evidence:** context-dependent Hsp70 responses in stressed murine neurons, including a 1–10 µM series and a 4 µM time course. The response depended on the model and sampling time. A separate rat ventral-horn experiment reported protection at a 1 µM condition. Neither study tested BubR1 or mitotic rescue. PMID 31900865, Figures 5–7 and Online Resource 2; PMID 19183864, Figures 1–3.
- **Translation anchor:** the US label covers NPC neurological manifestations with miglustat from age two. Estimated paediatric NPC steady-state mean serum trough/peak concentrations are 206/523 ng/mL. These are total serum values, not measured free intracellular exposure in our proposed system. [MIPLYFFA label §§1, 12](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5feffc0e-453d-47fa-91dd-38d4952309bc).
- **Counterevidence:** larger ALS and IBM trials did not demonstrate efficacy and reported tolerability concerns. EMA recommended refusal of the NPC application in July 2026; a re-examination request is recorded, without a final outcome established in our review. None of these observations tests MVA. PMID 38782015; PMID 37739573; [EMA procedure](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/meplyffa).
- **What changes our choice:** qualified experiments that rule out the prespecified worthwhile functional benefit at relevant exposure stop this drug–exposure–system combination. A repeatable advantage for another qualified candidate replaces this lead. A failed exposure measurement is indeterminate, not evidence against the drug. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

### 4-PBA: a credible comparator, not excluded by an ER-only argument

- **Positive evidence:** 1 mM 4-PBA transiently increased inducible Hsp70 through an Elp2/STAT3-associated pathway in CF epithelial cells. This was not a BubR1 experiment. Hsp70 and Hsc70 behaved differently; the Hsp70 response returned towards baseline by 24 hours. PMID 22069317, Figures 1–8.
- **Translation anchor:** sodium phenylbutyrate has a human urea-cycle-disorder indication. The BUPHENYL label reports phenylbutyrate Cmax 218 µg/mL after a single 5 g tablet dose in fasting normal adults, with a 0.77-hour elimination half-life. It states that pharmacokinetic studies were not conducted in the primary neonatal, infant and child population. A brief adult total-plasma peak cannot establish a sustained free concentration in dividing cells. We retain the label's mass units rather than assume the molecular-weight basis for the reported analyte. [BUPHENYL label, Clinical Pharmacology and Indications](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=463a36fa-3eb2-4326-8bd0-c8c7a11bca3a).
- **Counterevidence and limits:** the label identifies sodium-load and hepatic/renal cautions. The CF experiment establishes neither N1002K rescue nor a favourable benefit–risk balance in MVA. Conversely, the nominal 1 mM cell concentration alone is insufficient grounds to declare human exposure impossible. The earlier ER-only exclusion is withdrawn. **[OUR INFERENCE]**
- **What changes our choice:** a qualified, replicated functional result exceeding arimoclomol under candidate-specific exposure and fate constraints would favour 4-PBA. Comparing nominal concentrations or Hsp70 bands would not answer that question. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

### NMN: stronger target-abundance evidence, unresolved mutant function

- **Positive evidence:** North and colleagues measured increased BubR1 protein after NMN in cultured MEFs and aged WT mouse testes. This is more direct target-abundance evidence than we hold for arimoclomol. The published NMN conditions were 1 mM for 24 hours in MEFs and 500 mg/kg/day intraperitoneally for seven days in WT mice. Those are reported experimental conditions, not proposed human doses. The lifespan experiment used a SIRT2 transgene, not NMN or NR. PMID 24825348, Figure 6 and Methods.
- **Translation limit:** those experiments do not show that N1002K retains function, that NMN restores that function, or that the relevant exposure and safety conditions can be met in this child. NMN remains a research comparator in this programme. **[OUR INFERENCE]**
- **Counterevidence:** an NR implanted-tumour/metastasis study motivates scrutiny of the NAD axis but does not establish an NMN effect or quantify this child's risk. We do not equate NMN, NR and a SIRT2 transgene. PMID 36371959. **[OUR INFERENCE]**
- **What changes our choice:** preserved function at matched abundance followed by a replicated, exposure-qualified functional advantage could favour NMN. More BubR1 without qualifying function cannot. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

### Rapamycin / sirolimus: a downstream compensation hypothesis

- **Positive evidence and endpoint:** the main text of a Drosophila CIN study reports preservation of neuroblast numbers after rapamycin in bub3-depleted brains. This is a downstream cell-population endpoint, not full-length N1002K or mitotic rescue. Its rapamycin panel is Supplementary Figure 6C; that panel was not independently re-inspected in this comparison audit. PMID 41820377, Results, “Proteostasis failure and mitochondrial dysfunction contribute to the loss of stemness.”
- **Exposure:** the fly experiment used 40 µM in food for approximately 48 hours. That nominal food concentration does not establish internal exposure. The sirolimus label describes a 5–15 ng/mL whole-blood trough target for LAM, a different disease and compartment. Neither number is an MVA efficacy threshold or a proposed dose for this child. PMID 41820377, Methods; [sirolimus label §2.4](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=5908cd1a-fc5a-462f-99ed-1d8983e253c9&version=22).
- **Counterevidence and safety:** the earlier rapamycin observation in BubR1-hypomorphic mice was explicitly preliminary and unpublished, not a controlled published negative trial. The label warns of immunosuppression-related infection and malignancy risks and impaired wound healing. These do not quantify risk in MVA. PMID 23602569; [sirolimus label, boxed warning and §§5.1, 5.6](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=5908cd1a-fc5a-462f-99ed-1d8983e253c9&version=22).
- **What changes our choice:** a replicated improvement in useful tissue output, with qualified exposure and acceptable cell fate, could support a compensatory role even without correcting BubR1. Preserving cell numbers alone is insufficient to establish that benefit. **[OUR INFERENCE; PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

### Everolimus: human experience does not establish an MVA class effect

- **Positive evidence and functional limit:** human use supplies an established clinical record in other conditions. The reviewed sources do not demonstrate an everolimus-induced BubR1 increase, N1002K functional rescue or MVA tissue-output benefit. Neither the rapamycin fly result nor the preliminary mouse observation directly tests everolimus. [AFINITOR/AFINITOR DISPERZ label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=2150f73a-179b-4afc-b8ce-67c85cc72f04); PMID 41820377; PMID 23602569. **[OUR INFERENCE]**
- **Exposure:** the label targets a 5–15 ng/mL whole-blood trough for TSC-associated SEGA and partial-onset seizures. This clinical monitoring range is not a cell-culture concentration or an MVA efficacy threshold. [Label §2.8](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=2150f73a-179b-4afc-b8ce-67c85cc72f04).
- **Counterevidence and safety:** the label describes infections, pneumonitis, stomatitis, renal failure, impaired wound healing, metabolic abnormalities and myelosuppression. These disease-specific observations require scrutiny; they do not prove either superior safety or a universal contraindication in MVA. [Label §§5.1–5.10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=2150f73a-179b-4afc-b8ce-67c85cc72f04). **[OUR INFERENCE]**
- **What changes our choice:** candidate-specific evidence of a useful functional or tissue benefit, at qualified exposure with acceptable harms, could change priority. A presumed class effect is insufficient. A fair comparison with a BubR1-restoration programme ultimately requires a justified common tissue endpoint. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

### Functional rescue controls

UPCP peptide results in progeria and engineered BubR1 binding-rescue constructs provide different, target-proximal experimental precedents. They are useful for qualifying mechanistic controls, not evidence that an available repurposed medicine rescues N1002K. Our selected source-data reproductions check the published summaries; they are not new laboratory replication. PMID 37118121; PMID 39516273; [reanalysis](../examples/bubr1-source-reanalysis/README.md). **[OUR INFERENCE; OUR REANALYSIS]**

## A result that can defeat our preferred hypothesis

First establish full-length N1002K abundance and function, including a qualified abundance-matched WT comparison. If function is not retained at matched abundance, a simple abundance-restoration explanation loses support. A drug could still act through another mechanism, but that would require a separate hypothesis and evidence. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

For a qualified, rescuable system, compare vehicle, arimoclomol, exposure-qualified 4-PBA and research-only NMN using the same blinded functional and cell-fate endpoints. Use candidate-specific exposure schedules rather than identical nominal doses. Lock the worthwhile benefit, multiplicity handling and comparison contrasts before confirmation. Estimate direct treatment differences with confidence intervals; one significant vehicle comparison and one nonsignificant comparison do not prove that treatments differ. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Advancement requires functional benefit without unacceptable effects on division, death or selection. Results that fail measurement controls remain indeterminate. A positive result needs independent preparation, replication and a patient-relevant model, followed by a justified tissue-output endpoint. This design can favour another candidate or reject all tested candidates. No comparative efficacy result, accepting laboratory or complete project quotation is available.
