# Literature reading record

**23 October 2026: 33 main-article texts read in their available versions; 0 assessed papers remain abstract-only.** This count does not mean every figure, supplement or underlying dataset is completely reviewed. The [paper-level record](literature-reading-record.json) separates those components and states each gap.

The original 26 cited papers were reviewed across mechanism, therapeutics, clinical impact and methods. Three later arimoclomol trial/reanalysis papers, a 4-PBA counterexample, and three additional primary arimoclomol/stress-response papers expanded that work. These are AI-assisted source reviews, not independent clinical expert review or experimental replication.

## What counts as a read

A main-text read covers the available abstract, methods, results, discussion and end matter. Figure inspection and supplement coverage are recorded separately, including exact pages or tables where appropriate. A reference-list entry, metadata record, abstract, mined passage or successful DOI resolution never constitutes a full-paper read. Available author-manuscript versions can omit material present in the final publisher version; the 2010 Suijkerbuijk record identifies that specific methods gap.

The source pyramid uses Crossref deposited reference lists before GROBID extraction of the residue, followed by co-citation and tier-2 blind-spot discovery. Reference frequency prioritizes investigation; it does not create independent replications or establish relevance. Targeted searches for counterevidence complement the graph. Newly retrieved main texts are distinguished from duplicate extracts and failed access responses.

## How the reading changed the submission

The reviews corrected fragment thermal calibration and expression details, Q921H control interpretation, historical patient abundance ranges and haplotypes, an unsupported flow-cytometry precedent, and splice predictions presented as biological exclusions. The candidate comparison now includes direct NMN/BubR1 abundance evidence, 4-PBA stress-response evidence, negative larger arimoclomol trials and the dependence of the NPC reanalysis on its original trial.

The resulting protocol distinguishes an informative negative from an imprecise estimate, requires separate division/fate denominators, and separates concordant pathway measurements from causal mediation. Source errors and unresolved material remain visible rather than being converted into reassuring negative findings.
