# Functional rescue plan: full-length N1002K and exposure-limited arimoclomol

**Proposed experiments; not performed. Protocol revision: 23 October 2026.**

This is the decision protocol for the [Track 2 candidate](adriamasnou_track2_report.md). The fragment reagent specification remains an optional mechanistic sub-study. Neither a fragment melting curve nor increased BubR1 alone is sufficient to advance a drug.

## Primary question

Does arimoclomol reduce chromosome-segregation errors per completed division in an N1002K replacement system at a measured exposure compatible with the human pharmacokinetic anchor, without doing so through reduced division, cell death or selection? **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

## Stage 1: qualify the genotype model

Use a laboratory-validated full-length BUB1B replacement system with the same backbone, promoter, tag placement and endogenous depletion strategy across WT, N1002K and controls. Confirm sequence, expression and depletion; test the tag's effect with an orthogonal untagged or endogenous readout. Prespecify I909T or L1012P as a published low-abundance allele with forced-expression rescue of nocodazole checkpoint response. Do not assume this calibrates every endpoint. A separate function-separation control needs qualification for the chosen endpoint; Y155C is a published candidate, not an interchangeable thermal reference. Empty/depleted and WT-rescue controls establish dynamic range. Cells must be authenticated and mycoplasma-negative. A transformed-cell result needs confirmation in a relevant non-transformed or patient-derived system before translation. PMID 20516114. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Measure BUB1B transcript, full-length protein, turnover and kinetochore localisation. Quantify protein within a validated linear range and test normaliser stability. Compare N1002K both with ordinary WT rescue and with WT titrated to matched abundance. Choose a biologically justified matching margin that pilot precision can resolve. Feasible titration-range overlap is necessary but insufficient: confirm matching with a prespecified equivalence assessment of the abundance ratio or difference, whose uncertainty must lie within the matching bounds. If matching cannot be established, classify the comparison as indeterminate. Define a separate functional-equivalence margin before calling function retained; a nonsignificant difference does not establish equivalence. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Assignment is descriptive: low abundance with retained function when matched; reduced function despite matched abundance; both; or indeterminate. Q921H also had WT-like performance in the published cellular alignment, segregation and checkpoint assays; this is not fragment thermal qualification or proof of clinical benignity. Do not use its normal abundance as proof of a specific PP2A defect, and do not infer N1002K behaviour from domain membership. PMID 20516114; PMID 33207204. **[OUR INFERENCE]**

## Stage 2: exposure and response qualification

Proposed concentration series: vehicle, 0.2, 0.6, 1 and 2 µM arimoclomol; exploratory 4 and 10 µM reported separately. Sample multiple prespecified times, initially 6, 24 and 48 hours, to distinguish transient stress-response induction from sustained rescue. Kuta Online Resource 2 reports a 4 µM response at 6–24 hours that was absent at 48 hours; these proposed sampling times still require qualification in this cell system. They are not validated optima or human dosing instructions. PMID 31900865. Start with single-agent treatment; combinations require a separate hypothesis and control structure. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

The label gives estimated paediatric NPC serum trough/peak means equivalent to approximately 0.66/1.67 µM, not free intracellular concentrations. Measure medium stability and cellular exposure where possible and record protein content/binding. Record compound form, active-moiety basis, assay matrix, sampling time and total/free status for every exposure comparison. Use a pharmacology review before calling any tested concentration clinically reachable. Even 2 µM is a screening bracket, not a proven sustained therapeutic exposure. [MIPLYFFA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5feffc0e-453d-47fa-91dd-38d4952309bc); [calculation](../analysis/exposure_check.py).

Assay HSPA1A with BubR1 and a justified additional pathway readout. Include an independently qualified stress-response positive control on separate wells. A heat-shock control demonstrates assay capability; it must not be used to claim efficacy of the candidate under ordinary disease conditions. Cell type and initiating stress affect arimoclomol response. PMID 31900865. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

At each dose and time, interpret HSPA1A alongside injury and cell fate in drug-only WT and N1002K conditions; keep any imposed-stress control separate. HSPA1A induction accompanied by excess apoptosis is an adverse response to investigate, not a rescue hit. A rat neuronal study found Hsp70 staining alongside celastrol injury and arimoclomol-associated survival benefit under imposed stress; it supplies no acceptable injury threshold for this assay. PMID 19183864, Figures 1–3. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT; OUR INFERENCE]**

Freeze assay range, repeatability and positive-control acceptance criteria before interpreting an absent response. ALS biomarker limitations left HSP engagement unresolved; the IBM protocol removed HSP70 assays. Missing or unqualified measurements cannot establish a biological negative. PMID 38782015; PMID 37739573, supplementary Table S2.

With qualified controls and confirmed exposure, absent HSP response is negative evidence for the proposed mechanism. If function nevertheless improves, independently replicate it, exclude selection and investigate another mechanism; do not label it established HSP-mediated rescue. Reject for insufficient efficacy only when the upper confidence bound for benefit is below the prespecified worthwhile benefit; an interval touching or crossing that threshold is inconclusive. Failed measurement or exposure controls make interpretation technically indeterminate. HSP70 response alone neither proves direct binding nor establishes rescue. **[OUR INFERENCE]**

## Stage 3: prespecified outcomes and analysis

**Primary outcome:** fraction of completed divisions with a prespecified segregation error, scored from blinded time-lapse imaging. Define lagging chromosomes, bridges and multipolar divisions in an image-scoring manual before analysis. This is a conditional endpoint. Also report the starting tracked-cell population, mitotic entries, completed divisions, deaths, persistent arrests and censoring over a fixed follow-up window, with each denominator explicit. Prespecify repeated-division and incomplete-follow-up handling. Death or arrest is not recoded as a measured segregation error; a lower conditional error rate advances only if separate division-rate and fate criteria also pass. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

**Required secondary outcomes:** chromosome alignment, checkpoint response and mitotic duration; full-length BubR1 abundance/localisation; viability, apoptosis, division rate and cell-cycle distribution. A fall in micronuclei without a matched division/fate analysis is insufficient, because survival and proliferation can change the observed fraction. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Independent culture preparations are biological replicates. Cells and fields are nested observations, not independent n. Randomize treatments across plates and positions; blind imaging/scoring; record every excluded well and its prespecified technical reason. Use a hierarchical model or replicate-level estimates appropriate to the event counts. Before confirmation, lock the functional follow-up window, primary dose/vehicle contrast or comparison family, confidence level and role of each sampling time. If any dose–time contrast can trigger advancement, include all eligible contrasts in the multiplicity plan. Label other time-course analyses exploratory. Biomarker sampling and functional imaging windows may differ, but both must be specified before confirmatory results are examined. Report effect sizes and confidence intervals, not only p-values. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Before the pilot, freeze one readout-by-timepoint schedule specifying the measure, denominator, acceptance rule and decision for each observation. Verify that every required observation can be collected. Record missing images, cells and fields with reasons, alongside exclusions and cell fate; an uncollected assay is indeterminate. The ALS trial documented an intended endpoint that was not collected because its schedule was unclear (PMID 38782015, supplementary SAP §11.4). **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

A separate pilot estimates baseline error, overdispersion, usable divisions per culture and assay CV. Freeze a power calculation and confirmatory sample size before evaluating the candidate's confirmatory response. Three convenient wells do not establish adequate power. Do not derive cellular assay precision from interpatient PBMC variability in the NPC trial (PMID 34418116).

Before confirmation, the laboratory must register the smallest worthwhile reduction in segregation errors and acceptable proliferation/viability bounds. These are programme decisions to be justified by the pilot, not a claimed clinical benefit threshold. Define Δ as the reduction in segregation-error probability relative to vehicle and δ as the registered smallest worthwhile reduction. Apply the prespecified multiplicity-adjusted confidence interval [L,U]: L > δ supports the functional-benefit criterion; U < δ rules out the programme’s worthwhile benefit at that exposure; an interval touching or crossing δ is inconclusive. Separate proliferation/fate bounds must also pass. Neither a nonsignificant result nor a protein rise alone establishes a protein-only biological effect. These are programme criteria, not clinical thresholds.

The protocol is therefore **ready for laboratory design review, not fully preregistered or executable without that review**. The computational work cannot honestly supply missing pilot variance or clinical thresholds.

## Advancement rules

1. Advance only with reproducible functional benefit at qualified exposure and without cytostasis or selective killing explaining it. Concordant pathway, BubR1 and functional responses support mechanistic consistency, not causation. Claiming HSP-mediated rescue requires a controlled dependency/intervention experiment that separates pathway dependence from toxicity and nonspecific loss of fitness. Until then report a functional hit with a proposed mechanism; a discordant functional hit requires separate investigation.
2. Protein improvement without an adequate functional estimate cannot pass efficacy. Reject for insufficient efficacy only when U < δ; an interval touching or crossing δ remains inconclusive. A high-dose-only effect fails the current translational gate.
3. An informative drug negative does not prove the target class impossible. A failed control cannot support a biological negative.
4. Replicate the positive result independently in a relevant genotype model. Before any human investigation, require specialist clinical, oncology and clinical-pharmacology review, including renal/hepatic risk, current-label interactions and a justified monitoring plan. Adult trial doses and thresholds are not a paediatric MVA protocol. PMID 38782015; PMID 37739573. Include unresolved thrombotic observations from the earlier12-week ALS study in specialist benefit-risk review. Its proposed creatinine-transport explanation was not established; do not assume that a creatinine rise is benign or infer paediatric MVA safety from adult tolerability. PMID 18551622. **[PROPOSED TRANSLATION GATE; OUR INFERENCE]**
5. Publish every outcome category and its denominator, including inconclusive assays and failed concentrations. No patient treatment is proposed within this competition. **[OUR INFERENCE; PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

## Comparative branch

Do not preselect a winner by protein abundance alone. NMN has direct BubR1-abundance evidence in MEFs and aged WT mouse testes, without N1002K functional rescue (PMID 24825348). 4-PBA has a transient Hsp70 response at 1 mM in CF epithelial cells, without BubR1 measurement; Hsp70 and Hsc70 must be distinguished (PMID 22069317). If these alternatives enter the screen, qualify each exposure window and early/late response before comparing the same division-level outcomes. TUDCA lacks a demonstrated rescue result here. A negative high-dose experiment or a compartment label cannot categorically eliminate these mechanisms. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT; OUR INFERENCE]**
