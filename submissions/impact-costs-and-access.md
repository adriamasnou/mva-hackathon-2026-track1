# Costs, access and what this proposal does not yet fund

**23 October 2026 · planning context, not a clinical or financial offer**

## Laboratory programme

The existing fragment reagent specification contains dated vendor-price estimates for an initial biophysical experiment. Its approximate $630 materials figure excludes full-length cellular model development, assay qualification, labour, equipment access, pharmacokinetic measurements, independent replication and patient-derived validation. It cannot be presented as the cost of proving drug rescue. [Specification](n1002k-reagent-specification.md). **[ESTIMATE]**

The [functional plan](functional-rescue-plan.md) requires a laboratory to price those components separately after design review. No quote, sponsor, laboratory commitment or guaranteed completion time is held. Earlier figures of £16,000–£26,000 for a “whole programme” do not establish the cost of this revised programme and are not used to claim affordability. No numerical probability of a hit or treatment is justified by the desk work.

## Surveillance burden

The 2021 SIOP-Europe consensus recommendation of renal ultrasound every three months until the seventh birthday implies roughly 28 scheduled scans from birth, subject to how start and boundary visits are counted (PMID 34134020). A previous illustrative cost model used £79.07–£110.22 per scan and assumed 14 outpatient attendances at £339.90 each. Its arithmetic is £6,972.56–£7,844.76; these are historical unit-cost assumptions, not current tariffs, a family bill or a demonstrated MVA cost-effectiveness ratio. **[ESTIMATE; OUR MEASUREMENT]**

Travel, childcare, time away from work and waiting for results are additional burdens. Costs and preferences belong in discussion with the clinical team. Comparing surveillance costs to a transplant does not establish the benefit of surveillance and is not part of the current argument.

A historical Beckwith–Wiedemann model combined Wilms tumour and hepatoblastoma surveillance with four-monthly abdominal ultrasound (PMID 11568898). Its disease risks, assumed tumour-stage shifts and costs in 2000 US dollars do not establish present-day MVA cost-effectiveness. The favourable model result depends partly on the assumed hepatoblastoma benefit; it cannot be transferred directly to renal surveillance in MVA.

## From a laboratory result to a possible clinical study

Arimoclomol has no established MVA indication. A successful cell experiment would need replication, relevant-model validation, an exposure/safety assessment and specialist clinical and oncology review before consideration of human research. A sponsor, approvals, consent and outcome plan would be needed for a clinical study. This project has not secured any of them. **[PROPOSED TRANSLATION PATH]**

Routine funding mechanisms must not be presented as promises for an experimental proposal. The retained NHS England IFR policy distinguishes a conditional trial of treatment from work more appropriately conducted as research; availability must be checked by the treating service under current policy. An individualized ASO protocol is not evidence of an access route for a repurposed small molecule. No application, referral or request for treatment has been made.

The directly reusable deliverables are public code, a source-traceable register lookup and research communication. Their delivery cost is not a proxy for the cost or benefit of a future therapy.
