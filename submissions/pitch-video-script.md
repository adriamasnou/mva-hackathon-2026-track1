## The script (~460 words ≈ 3:00 at a measured pace)

### [0:00–0:22] — COLD OPEN. *On screen: the variant pair, plain text, no branding.*

> A child has mosaic variegated aneuploidy. Two *BUB1B* variants, in trans: one truncating, one
> missense. The diagnosis was already made. So the interesting question is not *what is the
> variant* — it is **what happens next, and whether the things that could help this family are
> findable at all.**

### [0:22–1:05] — IMPACT. *On screen: the HFEA register, then the count.*

*(Sources for this block: HFEA approved-conditions workbook, snapshot **2026-08-05**, parsed
directly — `repo/submissions/hfea-pgtm-gene-crosswalk.md`, retained at
`data/derived/hfea_pgtm_2026-08-05.xlsx`; the register audit in `repo/submissions/register-audit.md`,
regenerated by `pipeline/register_expert_curation.py`; the register and gene crosswalk are directly inspectable in the cited artifacts.)*

> We went looking for what already exists. **The UK regulator has already approved preimplantation
> genetic testing for mosaic variegated aneuploidy** — not once, but **five separate entries** on the
> HFEA's approved-conditions register.
> **[OUR MEASUREMENT — HFEA approved-conditions workbook, snapshot 2026-08-05, parsed directly;
> https://www.hfea.gov.uk/ ; crosswalk at `repo/submissions/hfea-pgtm-gene-crosswalk.md`, copy
> retained at `data/derived/hfea_pgtm_2026-08-05.xlsx`.]**
>
> That register lists **2,063 approved conditions**. It is searchable by **condition name**. It is
> not searchable by **gene**. So a clinician who knows this child has *BUB1B* has no route from the
> gene to the approval through a gene-search field. Our crosswalk supplies that missing route.
>
> We built the crosswalk. And building it exposed something larger: for **1,850 of those 2,063
> approved conditions — 89.7 percent —** there is **no expert-curated variant in any causative gene**
> in ClinVar. **The approval exists. The interpretation infrastructure behind it does not.**

### [1:05–1:50] — RIGOR. *On screen: the pipeline, then the pre-registration.*

> On the genomics: we re-annotated the child's entire callset — **313,417 variants** — with Ensembl
> VEP against the full cache, and we checked that nothing was dropped: 313,417 in, **313,417
> distinct out**.
>
> The gene ranking was **pre-registered before we looked**, and it is rebuildable from the raw VCF
> today: **230 candidate genes, *BUB1B* at rank 55.** We publish the three post-hoc variants of that
> rule beside it, and we publish that rank 55 sits inside **a tie block of eight** — because a rank
> is a position in a tie, not a measurement.

### [1:50–2:15] — SCALABILITY. *On screen: HG002.*

> Does it generalise? We ran the same pipeline, unchanged, on a **public** genome — GIAB HG002,
> **4,096,123 variants**, with a known answer spiked in. **It returned *FGFR2* at rank 1.** Same
> code, different case, public data, reproducible by anyone.

### [2:15–2:50] — INNOVATION, AND THE HONEST PART. *On screen: three struck-through claims.*

> On therapy we did mechanism-matching rather than list-making — which mostly told us what **not** to
> test.
>
> And here is what we want judged. **We are not submitting our variant to ClinVar.** We built the
> package, then found that three of the four criteria we had claimed do not hold. **One remains.
> So it does not go.** Our leading supplement candidate carries **an unresolved cancer signal in
> mice** — that is now in the family-facing document, not just our notes. And a mosaicism result we
> had held for three days turned out to be **mis-mapping**; we replaced it with a bounded negative.
>
> **We are showing you the retractions on purpose.**

### [2:50–3:00] — CLOSE. *On screen: nothing. Just the line.*

> The answer to this child's question already existed. **It could not be found.** That is the
> problem we worked on.
