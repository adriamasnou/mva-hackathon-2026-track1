# Three-minute pitch: recording script

**177 seconds (2:57).** Neutral synthetic narration and silent visuals use the same timing. These claims describe proposed research and measured software checks, not drug rescue.

## 00:00:00–00:00:25 — slide 1

For a child with mosaic variegated aneuploidy, a plausible medicine is only a starting point. We propose arimoclomol for laboratory testing, with explicit rules for deciding whether it deserves to advance. We have not demonstrated drug rescue or treated this child. Our contribution is a testable proposal, supported by working tools that others can use.

## 00:00:25–00:00:54 — slide 2

The two identified variants affect the gene that makes Bub R one, a protein involved in chromosome separation. Their phase is inferred. The exact protein consequences remain unmeasured. The missense variant could reduce abundance, impair function, do both, or remain indeterminate. We compare full-length normal and altered protein at matched abundance. An isolated fragment's melting curve cannot settle that question.

## 00:00:54–00:01:35 — slide 3

Arimoclomol has a human approval in a different disease. Its reported effects depend on the cell and the stress. That makes exposure a gate, not a footnote. First, qualify the model and measurement. Second, test whether the proposed response occurs at relevant exposure. Third, require better chromosome segregation, while tracking division, survival and cell fate. More protein alone does not pass. Neither does an apparent improvement caused by stopping division or selectively losing abnormal cells. Failed controls are indeterminate; a valid drug negative stops that tested approach.

## 00:01:35–00:02:04 — slide 4

We also built a practical gene-to-condition navigator. Search a gene, inspect its candidate condition mappings, and follow the source evidence. It includes every row of a dated, two thousand and sixty-three row register snapshot, including thirty-five unmapped rows and source disagreements. A match is not an eligibility decision, and no match is not evidence of ineligibility. Queries stay on the device.

## 00:02:04–00:02:32 — slide 5

Our interpretation canary checks that a model has actually answered. Real Splice A I inference passes six controls. A separate checker self-test rejects nine injected failures, including missing and nonfinite output. We distinguish those tests from clinical accuracy. A public-genome spike-in also demonstrates scale, while leaving diagnostic performance and knowledge leakage unresolved.

## 00:02:32–00:02:57 — slide 6

The next step is a laboratory test of full-length variant function and exposure-limited rescue. The proposal says what would advance the candidate and what would stop it. The reusable tools, source trail and plain-language explanation are delivered now. Clinical benefit remains to be earned. That is the standard we want this work judged against.
