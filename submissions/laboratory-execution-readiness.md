## First release: establish whether the allele has a measurable defect

Start with the [full-length functional plan](functional-rescue-plan.md) and [sequence specification](n1002k-reagent-specification.md). Retain an economical transient replacement feasibility pilot and a separately priced stable-clone alternative. Qualify endogenous depletion, WT functional rescue, measured protein abundance, identity/mycoplasma and imaging. Transient expression needs delivery/abundance stratification and cannot replace independent stable-model confirmation. Ten stable clones are not an assumed first purchase.

A proposed four-well vehicle-only optical preflight uses WT replacement, N1002K replacement, depleted empty and nondepleted WT, one clone/culture each. This tests resolution, phototoxicity and throughput; it is not an efficacy experiment or a powered defect comparison. The subsequent proposed expression titration is 240 wells. Stop if WT rescue fails or the mutant comparison cannot be interpreted. Exact vector/duplex/junction designs and release criteria require the executing laboratory's acceptance.

Only then consider the arimoclomol pilot: **756 main + 27 process + 24 separate stress-control wells = 807**, before exposure, development and repeats. The main layout has 18 plates, 42 main wells each. Three independent culture blocks estimate feasibility/variance, not confirmatory efficacy. Exposure scope is 276 samples before calibration/QC. Confirmation needs frozen effect/fate margins, cluster-aware power and a separate quote. Broad LCL screening, protein production and tissue extensions are optional stages, not first-release requirements.

**Imaging is a critical unresolved dependency.** Forty-two wells × eight fields × two channels means 336 positions/672 images per proposed 180 second cycle. A provider must demonstrate exposure, autofocus, stage/channel switching, resolution and phototoxicity on the actual platform. Six 48 h runs on a single-plate instrument require counterbalanced staggered culture blocks; a multi-position platform needs demonstrated capacity. Otherwise reduce the pilot and recalculate. No 3 min cadence, feasible six-run schedule or imaging-service price is promised.

The [CRG access policy](https://www.crg.eu/sites/default/files/crg/almu_access_policy.pdf), updated 3 February 2026 and retrieved 24 October, requires staff training, suggests requesting it at least two weeks ahead, and normally permits at most three live-cell overnight bookings per person per week. If each 48 h run uses two overnight bookings, six runs require 12 bookings across **at least four weekly quota periods for one operator**. This is a conditional scheduling inference: booking definitions, calendar capacity, exceptions and project acceptance are unconfirmed. It is not a 12-consecutive-day schedule or a promised completion time. No exception or booking has been requested.
