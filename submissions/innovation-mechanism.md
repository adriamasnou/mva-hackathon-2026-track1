# From an uncertain allele to an interpretable experiment

**23 October 2026 · adriamasnou · research proposal**

The [main report](adriamasnou_track2_report.md) proposes arimoclomol as a conditional preclinical lead. Its value depends on three measurements: whether full-length N1002K retains rescuable function, whether the proposed response is engaged at relevant exposure, and whether segregation improves without cytostasis or selection. Neither a domain prediction nor a protein increase supplies those measurements. PMID 20516114; PMID 22698286; PMID 33207204. **[OUR INFERENCE]**

The [functional rescue protocol](functional-rescue-plan.md) separates abundance-limited, function-limited, mixed and indeterminate results, using prespecified equivalence margins for matching/retained function. Its confidence-interval rule distinguishes evidence excluding a worthwhile benefit from an imprecise result; no significance is not evidence of no benefit. WT titrated to the mutant's abundance helps distinguish amount from function. An isolated-domain thermal assay is supportive only; normal fragment stability does not exclude full-length defects. No N1002K rescue result is claimed.

Arimoclomol's response varies by experimental context. Low-micromolar effects in stressed mouse neurons do not demonstrate rescue of BubR1 in a dividing human cell. The exposure comparison and earlier EMA concerns are presented in the main report. With adequate precision, a failed, technically valid engagement result is evidence against the proposed mechanism; it must not be relabelled as assay failure to protect the hypothesis. Functional benefit without the expected HSP response requires replication and separate mechanism investigation. Negative larger ALS and IBM trials make this separation material, not merely procedural. PMID 38782015; PMID 37739573. PMID 31900865. **[OUR INFERENCE]**

The delivered innovation is also practical: [an offline public-register navigator](pgtm-navigator.html) with per-gene source evidence and explicit unresolved mappings, and [a portable interpretation canary](../canary/README.md) that rejects missing output and can demonstrate its own detection of injected faults. The [navigator task audit](pgtm-task-validation.md) exercises seven selected gene–condition targets and four ambiguity/absence cases against saved source evidence and the actual HTML query implementation. Seven targets were retrieved versus three by literal title matching; this small, unblinded convenience evaluation is not clinical accuracy or a live-service comparison. Family usability, improved access and clinical benefit remain untested.

We make no priority or exclusivity claim for arimoclomol, proteostasis, abundance/function distinctions or positive controls. The contribution is their implementation and explicit decision boundaries for this case, with reusable public tools. Earlier blanket compartment exclusions, molecule “kill” claims and subjective success probabilities have been withdrawn. Clinical PBMC variability is not cellular assay precision. The corrected report is the current interpretation; Git history retains the prior drafts.
