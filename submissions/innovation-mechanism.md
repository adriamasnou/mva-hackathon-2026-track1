# From an uncertain allele to an interpretable experiment

**23 October 2026 · adriamasnou · research proposal**

The [main report](adriamasnou_track2_report.md) proposes arimoclomol as a conditional preclinical lead. Its value depends on three measurements: whether full-length N1002K retains rescuable function, whether the proposed response is engaged at relevant exposure, and whether segregation improves without cytostasis or selection. Neither a domain prediction nor a protein increase supplies those measurements. PMID 20516114; PMID 22698286; PMID 33207204. **[OUR INFERENCE]**

The [functional rescue protocol](functional-rescue-plan.md) separates abundance-limited, function-limited, mixed and indeterminate results. WT titrated to the mutant's abundance helps distinguish amount from function. An isolated-domain thermal assay is supportive only; normal fragment stability does not exclude full-length defects. No N1002K rescue result is claimed.

Arimoclomol's response varies by experimental context. Low-micromolar effects in stressed mouse neurons do not demonstrate rescue of BubR1 in a dividing human cell. The exposure comparison and earlier EMA concerns are presented in the main report. A failed, technically valid drug-engagement result is informative for that drug and system; it must not be relabelled as an assay failure to protect the hypothesis. PMID 31900865. **[OUR INFERENCE]**

The delivered innovation is also practical: [an offline public-register navigator](pgtm-navigator.html) with per-gene source evidence and explicit unresolved mappings, and [a portable interpretation canary](../canary/README.md) that rejects missing output and can demonstrate its own detection of injected faults. These are usable artifacts. Family usability, improved access and clinical benefit remain untested.

We make no priority or exclusivity claim for arimoclomol, proteostasis, abundance/function distinctions or positive controls. The contribution is their implementation and explicit decision boundaries for this case, with reusable public tools. Earlier blanket compartment exclusions, molecule “kill” claims and subjective success probabilities have been withdrawn. Clinical PBMC variability is not cellular assay precision. The corrected report is the current interpretation; Git history retains the prior drafts.
