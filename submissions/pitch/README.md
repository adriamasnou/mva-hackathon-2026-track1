# Three-minute pitch kit

Two local versions share the same six visuals and 2:57 timeline:

- `pitch-narrated.mp4`: 1080p H.264/AAC, neutral synthetic English narration (macOS Samantha), with optional English subtitle track.
- `pitch-silent.mp4`: the same visuals without audio, ready for your narration.
- `pitch-editable.pptx`: editable text and the actual navigator screenshot.
- `pitch-slides.pdf`: matching static visuals.
- [Timed recording script](../pitch-video-script.md) and `timing.json`: segment boundaries.
- `pitch-captions.srt`: editable captions. Timing is estimated from synthesized sentence/word lengths; review alignment before publishing.

The spoken script is 369 words. The synthetic version is a reviewable alternative, not a recording of the participant. No voice cloning or impersonation is used. For your version, record the timed script while watching the silent video. Keep the export under three minutes and verify any edited scientific statement against the report. Review pronunciation and captions when choosing the final version.

The files are local. Neither version has been hosted or submitted, so no external pitch URL exists yet. The visual snapshot shows a real BUB1B lookup, not a simulated lab result. Claims of therapeutic rescue or clinical benefit are deliberately absent.
