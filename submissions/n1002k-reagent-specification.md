## Verified insert designs

The portable [construct specifications](../examples/laboratory-readiness/construct-specifications.json) contain public-reference-derived insert sequences, lengths, intended changes and SHA256 hashes. The reference is [NM_001211.6](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/NM_001211.6), retrieved 24 October 2026: CDS length 3,153 nucleotides, encoding 1,050 residues plus stop. No full reference transcript or patient input is included. These are insert designs, **not assembled vectors**. The versioned Ensembl route failed during this refresh; it is not a second fresh sequence confirmation. **[OUR SEQUENCE AUDIT]**

WT codon 1002 is AAT. **c.3006T>G gives AAG; c.3006T>A gives AAA.** Both encode N1002K but remain distinct nucleotide alleles. The base programme concerns T>G. A cDNA assay cannot resolve native splicing, transcript regulation, allele phase or the full clinical genotype. **[OUR SEQUENCE AUDIT; PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Required coding designs for the proposed control panel are WT, N1002K(T>G), I909T(c.2726T>C design) and Y155C(c.464A>G design), plus a matched empty backbone. Optional JSON designs are N1002K(T>A), L1012P, Q921H, K795A, T600A/T608A(2A), and 2A+D675E/S676E/R677E(3E). Comparator nucleotide choices encode the intended proteins; they do not assert that source authors used those exact codons. Do not treat the controls as clinically calibrated pathogenic/benign standards. I909T helps examine abundance and forced expression; Y155C helps separate abundance from function; neither establishes N1002K's defect. PMID 20516114; PMID 22698286. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

The four optional aa 721–1044 fragments are 972 nt/324 residues: WT, N1002K(T>G), K795A and Q921H. They omit initiation/termination, vector, tag and junctions. N1002K changes fragment nucleotide 846. All four were matched to the fresh public CDS derivation. The proposed mutagenesis forward primer is `CTTTGTGCGGATTCTGAAGGCCAATGATGAGGCCAC` (36 nt), reverse `GTGGCCTCATCATTGGCCTTCAGAATCCGCACAAAG`. Reverse complementarity and the mutant CDS match were checked; a primer sequence is not a qualified mutagenesis reaction. **[OUR SEQUENCE AUDIT]**

## Provider release requirements

Request a shared inducible mammalian vector, preferably untagged BubR1, with identical backbone and expression control across variants. The provider must return promoter/inducer, insertion system, selectable marker, reporter/tag, start/stop and complete junction sequences for review. Any tag or stop-codon change requires translation and functional/localisation qualification. Supply complete plasmid sequence/QC and transfection-grade DNA, with endotoxin specification. No complexity tier, assembly success or delivery date is accepted yet. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Quote an economical **transient replacement feasibility pilot** separately from a stable-clone route. Transient heterogeneity and overexpression must be measured; abundance-matched single-cell strata and WT depletion-rescue controls are essential, and early feasibility is not stable-model confirmation. Ten stable clones are a separately priced later design, not a prerequisite purchase for the first feasibility check. Banked stable models require identity, mycoplasma, insertion/copy-number and expression QC, two independently derived clones/design if that route advances. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

The published printed RNA strand `GUCUCACAGAUUGCUGCCUCAGAGC` is 25 nt and matches reference mRNA 3319–3343 in the **3′UTR**, outside the CDS. A CDS-only replacement without that UTR lacks the exact target. Require both duplex strands, overhangs/chemistry and a second independent 3′UTR design from the supplier; the printed strand is not a complete 27-mer order. Check all final targets against the delivered cassette and verify endogenous depletion separately from replacement expression. Historical silent CDS changes do not establish resistance to an arbitrary depletion design. **[OUR SEQUENCE AUDIT; PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

[Addgene 114052](https://www.addgene.org/114052/) is an alternative deposited mammalian full-length tagged construct route, not an interchangeable bacterial fragment or full-length GST purification system. [Addgene 23858](https://www.addgene.org/23858/) is a Gateway entry clone without stop codon and needs an appropriate destination construct. Use provider maps and actual delivered sequence, not catalogue names alone. Availability/licensing and suitability remain conditional.
