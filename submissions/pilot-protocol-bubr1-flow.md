## Specificity, linearity and cell handling

Use independently confirmed BUB1B depletion with orthogonal WB in a qualified adherent model, then qualify delivery in the LCLs; nucleofection may be necessary and is not included by buying RNAiMAX. The published 25 nt RNA strand is not a fully specified purchasable duplex. The [construct record](../examples/laboratory-readiness/construct-specifications.json) maps it to the public 3′UTR; both strands, overhangs/chemistry and a second independent target remain required. Candidate concentrations 0.1/1/10 nM at 48/72 h, 20 nM only if justified by qualification, are pilot settings. Include NC1, mock, delivery and HPRT controls. Measure endogenous transcript and independent-epitope protein, and distinguish signal loss from cell injury. Suggested ≥ 50% flow-signal loss with verified depletion is a proposed engineering gate. PMID 33207204; [IDT guidance](https://www.idtdna.com/pages/products/functional-genomics/dsirnas-and-trifecta-rnai-kits). **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Linearity/recovery pilot: characterize high/low abundance populations independently, then mix 0/25/50/75/100% at equal viable input. Assess distributions and expected mixture behaviour; the median need not vary linearly with mixture fraction. Use fluorescence calibration beads/day controls. Primary population is all viable singlets; DNA strata are secondary, with no forced normal 2N/4N gate on an aneuploid LCL. Unrelated-protein controls do not alone prove the mechanism of a protein increase. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

## Corrected precision experiment

Twelve distinct conditions: GM21284 unstained, isotype, secondary-only and baseline; GM12878 baseline; GM18502 baseline; matched GM21284 vehicle/MG132 at 2, 4, 6 h. Proposed MG132 10 µM and common tolerated vehicle. MG132 is an abundance-accumulation control, not functional rescue; score injury. The current [Sigma M7449](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/m7449) stock is 10 mM DMSO. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

Twelve technical replicates per condition = 144 samples per day/lot. Each of **three 96-well plates** contains four replicates of every condition in randomized inner wells: 48 samples/plate, 12 spare inner positions, perimeter buffer. Two independently prepared days × two antibody lots = 576 samples; six separate compensation tubes/day-lot add 24. At 500,000 loaded cells/sample, 600 samples require 300 million cells, or 360 million with a 20% handling allowance. The [exact proposed layout](../examples/laboratory-readiness/flow-precision-layout.json) fits the allocation; titration, specificity, WB, alternative fix/perm and repeat failures are extra work. **[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT; ESTIMATE]**

At 5 µg/mL/50 µL, 432 primary-positive samples consume 108 µg primary, 129.6 µg with 20% handling. Omission/isotype controls do not consume BubR1 primary. Two distinct ≥ 100 µg lots are a proposed precision/development request; full-screen stock is separately costed. **[ESTIMATE]**

Acquire 25,000 events/well where feasible with a 20,000 retained-event engineering floor, and document recovery/gating/exclusions. These are acquisition goals, not universal median-precision guarantees. The 1.2533σ/√n expression assumes an approximately normal sampling setting; it is not distribution-free. The old χ² variance calculation with an estimated mean treated as fixed is an approximation, not an exact confidence bound on CV. Estimate repeatability, between-day/lot variance and uncertainty in the actual assay; technical n = 12 is not biological power. **[OUR INFERENCE; PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**

A 6.7% CV target remains conditional on the hypothetical 25%-to-50% contrast and equal-CV Z′ assumptions, not a measured N1002K baseline or clinical threshold. Freeze precision/control acceptance from qualification before screening. More wells can reduce uncertainty about repeatability but cannot repair nonspecificity, nonlinear measurement or inadequate biological dynamic range. Failure triggers assay revision or an independently qualified orthogonal method, not a biological negative. **[OUR INFERENCE; PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]**
