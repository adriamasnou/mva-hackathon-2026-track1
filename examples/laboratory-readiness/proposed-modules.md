# Proposed execution specification and release gates

**Status:** a local specification for provider review and laboratory qualification, not a validated SOP or authorization to begin. The independent proposal is currently unfunded. No cash-prize commitment is made; Claude Science credits apply only to eligible computation. No patient material is available. All numerical starting conditions introduced here are **proposed pilot settings** unless explicitly identified as inherited published or provider conditions.

## 1. Facility, model and sequence requirements

A host laboratory must accept the work, provide trained human-cell culture staff, biological safety cabinet, humidified 37°C/5% CO₂ incubator, refrigerated centrifuge, liquid-nitrogen banking, −20/−80°C storage, tissue-culture plastics, cell counting, STR and mycoplasma testing, validated hazardous-waste handling and appropriately calibrated instruments. These are dependencies with costs, not resources supplied by this project. No purchased vial or published “available on request” cell line establishes access.

First-release alternative: request a transient replacement feasibility pilot with matched coding constructs and empty control before committing to stable clones. Quantify delivery and expression heterogeneity, use abundance-matched cell-level strata, and require WT depletion-rescue qualification; transient overexpression cannot settle native allele effects or replace independent stable confirmation. The provider must reconcile its well count and controls. The five-design/two-clone plan below is a separately quoted stable route, not a required first purchase.

Stable-route base model: engineered **HeLa CCL-2** in one laboratory-qualified inducible integration system. Request a common low-expression chromosome reporter background for live segregation imaging, five matched designs (WT, N1002K c.3006T>G, I909T, Y155C, empty) and two independently derived clones per design. The provider must specify the actual vector, integration site/copy number, promoter/inducer, tag, reporter sequence, selectable marker and complete junctions before manufacture. Start from single-copy inducible, untagged BubR1 if the provider can supply and validate it; a tag is a documented alternative requiring functional WT rescue and localisation equivalence. The public T-REx/Flp-In gift system is a methodological precedent, not an available material. No vector sequence has been invented.

Incoming and final bank requirements: STR authentication; mycoplasma negative with valid positive/negative assay controls; complete plasmid sequence; genomic insertion/copy-number and transcript checks; growth/cell-cycle baseline; cryobank at least three vials per accepted clone. Record passage, thaw, medium lot, serum lot and provenance. Confirm that WT replacement rescues the depleted model before interpreting mutant/drug failure. Failed contamination, identity, sequence or rescue QC stops that line's experiments.

Optional independent context: **hTERT RPE-1 CRL-4000**, separately engineered and qualified, not a replication merely because the same plasmid is transfected. Native-locus c.3006T>G and optional T>A knock-ins require independent edited clones and WT editing controls, local genomic sequence/junction and RNA/allelic-expression verification. No patient allele phase or splice consequence follows from cDNA replacement.

LCL branch: **GM21284** (catalogued S738fsX753/R814H), **GM12878** and **GM18502** as independently sourced population-reference LCLs. References must themselves pass BubR1/growth qualification; “reference” does not mean measured normal BubR1. GM21654/55 are optional CEP57 disease comparators, not molecular specificity controls. Family-derived GM21657/GM22004 are not designated carriers without genotype evidence. Catalogue LCL starting conditions: RPMI, 2 mM final L-glutamine, 15% non-heat-inactivated FBS, 37°C/5% CO₂, split 1:4. Expand and bank with at least two post-thaw passages before assay qualification, subject to lab growth criteria.

## 2. Model qualification and abundance/function separation

Depletion pilot: compare NC1, mock, fluorescent delivery control, HPRT control and BUB1B duplex; proposed concentrations 0.1, 1, 10 nM at 48/72 h, with 20 nM only as a flagged escalation if lower doses fail without injury. The legacy 5/20/50 nM series is not adopted automatically. Measure endogenous BUB1B RNA using a qualified 3′UTR assay and BubR1 protein using independent-epitope WB. Quantify replacement RNA separately using a vector/CDS junction assay. Exact primer/probe sequences, efficiency and target discrimination are provider deliverables, not presumed from gene names. Choose the lowest effective dose with acceptable cell fate and document the choice before mutant/drug comparisons.

Proposed expression titration: 0, 1, 10, 100 ng/mL doxycycline **only if the provider's selected system uses doxycycline**; otherwise issue a revised, versioned titration. Five designs × four levels × target/NC1 × three independent culture preparations × live/lysate = **240 wells**, first clone per design. Plate 150 µL/well in a qualified 96-well format; start a density pilot at 2,000/5,000/10,000 viable cells per well, inspecting 6/24/48 h and selecting a density that permits observable divisions without confluence at 48 h. These densities are candidates, not measured HeLa capacity in the engineered model.

Determine cell-cycle-adjusted protein abundance, kinetochore localisation and function; use total-protein WB normalization and independently qualified loading controls. Compare N1002K to WT at overlapping measured abundance. Freeze an abundance-equivalence margin and an independent functional-equivalence margin justified by assay precision/biological relevance. Do not infer equality from nonsignificance. I909T is an abundance/forced-expression comparator; Y155C is a function-separation candidate, not a universal failure control. Q921H is optional WT-like cellular comparator, not a clinical benign standard.

Stop if depletion cannot be distinguished from generalized injury, WT cannot functionally rescue, expression/localisation cannot be matched, reporter or imaging causes mitotic injury, or the N1002K model has no interpretable defect. A negative mutant contrast can be informative; it does not authorize a drug-superiority claim or a clinical benign classification.

## 3. Arimoclomol pilot and analytical exposure

A proposed 10 mM DMSO stock uses 3.138 mg/mL; 1 mg would yield 318.7 µL. Supplier confirms DMSO solubility but gives no numeric solubility in the held insert, so clarity and analytical recovery at 10 mM must be qualified. Do not apply salt molecular weight or assume nominal dose equals exposure.

Vehicle, 0.2, 0.6, 1, 2 µM are the main window; 4 and 10 µM are explicitly exploratory. Use common final DMSO 0.1% if tolerated: prepare 1000 × dose stocks and a 100 × intermediate for practical dosing, then 1.5 µL intermediate into 148.5 µL medium. The vehicle undergoes identical handling. Inspect for precipitation and qualify actual medium recovery/stability. 6/24/48 h are separate endpoints. At 48 h, assay medium changes/redosing only if the stability study supports a prespecified schedule; do not quietly replace drug in one arm only.

The generated matrix is 2 genotypes × 2 clones × 7 doses × 3 durations × 3 independent culture blocks × 3 assays = **756 wells**. Each block/assay is balanced across two 96-well plates at 42 main wells/plate (60 inner-well capacity), making 18 main plates. The proposed map requires lab approval and a complete supplemental control layout. Additional process controls: nondepleted WT, depleted empty and NC1 empty × 3 blocks × 3 assays = 27 wells. Separate stress controls: 37°C/42°C 30 min with 2 h recovery × vehicle/2 µM arimoclomol × 3 blocks × engagement/fate assays = 24 wells. The heat setting is a proposed positive-control pilot, not a therapeutic condition and not required for rescue. Total 807 wells before general development, exposure sampling or repeats.

Assay split:

- **Live:** start imaging at treatment, stop at the assigned 6/24/48 h endpoint. Use temperature/CO₂ control, chromosome and transmitted-light channels, proposed 3 min intervals, eight preassigned fields/well; neither setting is accepted until measured throughput and phototoxicity pass. Select objective/resolution only after confirming visible lagging chromosomes/bridges and phototoxicity controls; a price for widefield use does not prove instrument suitability. Track all initially eligible cells in fields, all mitotic entries and all completed divisions. Target ≥ 100 starting cells/well and ≥ 20 completed divisions as pilot estimation goals; low completion is a result/fate issue, not permission to discard toxic wells or image selectively. Prespecify segmentation, adjudication and censoring before unblinding.
- **Lysate:** BubR1, inducible HSP70 protein with isoform limits stated, HSPA1A RT-qPCR and a second qualified pathway marker (e.g. DNAJB1); total-protein normalization. Catalogue HSP70 reactivity does not prove HSPA1A-only recognition. Archive raw blots, uncropped images, qPCR curves and sample identifiers. Reagent panels and quantities require lab quote.
- **Fixed:** chromosome alignment, kinetochore BubR1 normalized to a qualified centromere reference, B56/PLK1 recruitment where qualified, spindle/DNA morphology. Same-host antibody conflicts require separate wells or different host/direct conjugates. Report per-cell as well as aggregate distributions; fixed localization is not a direct phosphorylation measurement.

Primary proposed endpoint: segregation errors per **completed division**, accompanied by starting-cell count, mitotic entry, completion, arrest, death, apoptosis, censoring and repeat-division counts. Repeated divisions of the same lineage are not independent biological replicates. Mitotic duration is a distinct endpoint; timing improvement alone is not SAC-silencing rescue. Clinical trough/peak total serum concentrations do not establish free medium or intracellular concentration.

## 5. Replication, analysis, power and stop rules

The three culture blocks in the pilots estimate feasibility and variance; **they are not a powered efficacy experiment**. Randomize within block/plate, balance genotypes/doses/clone positions, blind acquisition/adjudication, and retain all exclusions with reasons before unblinding. Biological independence is culture preparation/day; clone is an additional design factor, and cells/fields/wells are nested. A model that counts thousands of cells as thousands of experiments is unacceptable.

Before confirmation, freeze the primary contrast/time/dose, the minimum worthwhile benefit δ, allowable excess death/arrest/noncompletion bounds, abundance-equivalence and functional-equivalence margins, family of comparisons, analysis model and missing/censoring policy. Select margins for biological meaning and assay capability, not from observed significance. Use a cluster-aware binomial or culture-summary analysis with paired blocks and clone effects; blinded manual adjudication must verify image classification. Provide absolute risk differences and CIs, not only p-values or folded normalized means.

`power-scenarios.json` is a prospective normal-approximation calculator for independent paired culture differences at 90% power/two-sided 5% alpha. It shows how pilot variance and the gap between expected effect and δ alter n. It does not account for small-n t uncertainty, multiplicity or missing cultures; final confirmation requires simulation with those features, a justified inflation for attrition and a budget amendment. A planning minimum of six blocks in scenarios is a design safeguard, not proof of adequate power. No new significance test was performed here.

Advance only if the benefit CI lower bound exceeds δ **and** exposure, viability, completion and arrest gates pass. If upper bound is below δ, reject a worthwhile benefit in that tested setting. Otherwise record inconclusive. In particular, a lower error proportion among very few surviving divisions is not rescue. Head-to-head superiority requires a prespecified pairwise advantage exceeding its margin with multiplicity handled. No experiment defined here establishes universal superiority over untested competitors or guarantees a clinical outcome.

## 6. LCL flow qualification and primary screen

Use a frozen, internally consistent panel. Proposed base: unconjugated 8G1 (NB100-353, mouse IgG1κ) with highly cross-adsorbed anti-mouse Alexa488, Near-IR fixable viability applied before fixation, and an instrument-qualified DNA dye channel. Supplier's HeLa example used direct DyLight650, 4% PFA, 0.1% saponin, 5 µg/mL 30 min RT; switching to indirect detection/LCLs is an adaptation. Proposed fixation 20 min RT and washing 350 g 5 min require recovery qualification. Do not retain the old 90% methanol or 1% Triton condition without a separately labelled comparison. No simultaneous DM1A same-mouse-secondary signal is interpreted as BubR1; unrelated protein goes in a separate well/blot or qualified different-host panel. DAPI/AF350 incompatibility must not be hidden by a renamed channel.

Start with antibody 1.25/2.5/5/10 µg/mL at 50 µL, n = 3 technical wells per cell type/condition for background and recovery. Include unstained, primary-omitted, concentration-matched **IgG1κ** isotype, single-color compensation and fluorescence-minus-one controls where needed. Confirm specificity by independent BUB1B depletion with WB in a qualified adherent system and then qualify LCL delivery (nucleofection may be necessary). Confirm flow loss follows target depletion, not death or cell-size shifts. Suggested ≥ 50% flow loss with independently confirmed depletion is an engineering gate, not a validated assay specification yet.

Linearity: prepare five known mixtures of high- and low-BubR1 cells (0/25/50/75/100% after separate characterization), maintaining equal viable cell input; assess distributions and expected mixture behaviour, not only a forced linear fit to median fluorescence. Include calibrated fluorescence beads and day-to-day normalization. Use all viable singlets as the primary population; DNA-content strata are secondary. An aneuploid line must not be forced into a normal 2N/4N gate.

Precision layout is explicit in `flow-precision-layout.json`: 12 conditions × 12 technical replicates per day/lot. Each of three plates contains four replicates of every condition in randomized inner wells; 48 samples/plate, 12 spare inner wells, perimeter buffer. Repeat across two days and two antibody lots: 576 samples plus 24 separate compensation tubes. Conditions comprise unstained/isotype/secondary-only/baseline GM21284, both reference baselines and matched MG132 10 µM/vehicle at 2/4/6 h. MG132 is an abundance-accumulation control and may injure cells; it is not rescued function. At 500,000 cells per sample the precision run alone loads 300 million cells including compensation, 360 million with 20% handling allowance. Additional development and WB are outside that count.

Acquire 25,000 events where feasible and retain a 20,000-event engineering floor after gates; neither value guarantees a universal median precision. Estimate repeatability, between-day/lot variance and uncertainty using appropriate replicate-level methods. The old χ² calculation treating an estimated CV denominator as fixed is not an exact CV bound. Technical n = 12 does not confer biological power. The old 6.7% CV target is conditional on an assumed assay separation, not measured N1002K biology. Freeze acceptance from qualified effect size and the planned Z′/precision objective.

## 8. Schedule and accountable handoff

There is no promised completion date. Procurement/engineering is a critical path with unconfirmed provider acceptance. Generic published stable-cell service starts from 7 weeks; this is neither an N1002K commitment nor a deadline.
