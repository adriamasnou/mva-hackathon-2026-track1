Definitions and interpretation

What is counted

Counts refer to source-label recovery under the frozen benchmark rule. A source label is the recorded benchmark target, not independently established clinical truth. HPO means Human Phenotype Ontology; the original arm uses the frozen original HPO input and the no-HPO arm omits it.

Each dimension is a separate marginal partition of its cohort. Categories from different dimensions must not be added or treated as independent observations. Failures remain in every relevant denominator.

What source categories mean

“Supported”, “qualified”, “contradicted” and “unresolved” are preserved source-correspondence annotations. They are not diagnostic verdicts. “Complete” records coverage, not universal support. A contradicted packet can reflect phenotype-only or formal-classification disagreement; it does not mean a disproved diagnosis.

No source record or a pending selected-packet review maps to unreviewed, regardless of its raw template labels. Those raw labels remain preserved in the joined case data. “None recorded” means no contradicted phenotype item was recorded in an adjudicated selected packet; it is not proof that no discrepancy exists.

Selected-packet dimensions concern the frozen selected packet. Completion dimensions include every linked selected and alternate packet or paper, according to the definitions printed above each table.

Limits that remain

    All adverse results remain inspectable. For example, no-HPO top 5 is 300/316 versus 297/316 for incomplete linked-paper coverage, 300/308 versus 297/308 for unresolved selected packets, and 147/153 versus 146/153 for genotype-supported packets. In the fixed 64-pair cohort, no-HPO top 1 is 59/64 versus 58/64. These are descriptive counts, not causal estimates.

    Reproduce this companion offline

    From the extracted package directory, using Python 3.11 or newer:

    python3 -B verify.py

    The verifier hashes the complete package, runs the original 29 tests and eight packaging integrity controls, regenerates five deterministic outputs in a temporary directory and compares their exact bytes. It reruns the unchanged parent's independent calculation of all 713 cases, 1,426 arm rows and 162 categories. It also compares this inline data with the complete frozen JSON. No inference, network request or article review is performed; the package remains unchanged.

    The original raw-benchmark verification receipt is included and hash-checked. The full raw inference-output bundle is outside this companion; its original raw-output verification is not rerun here. Node.js, when installed, additionally checks script syntax and all selector combinations. Browser visual review is a separate step.
